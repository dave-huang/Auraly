Goal:
Improve the visual hierarchy, clarity, and engagement of the existing first-time welcome modal while preserving its calm, trustworthy tone. Lead with new welcome copy and enhance layout to better highlight value, reduce anxiety, and encourage users to proceed.

⸻

1. Replace Intro Copy

Update the top copy of the modal to:

Welcome to Auraly! As a first-time member, you’re getting a 7-day complimentary upgrade to Auraly+ with full access to all features.

Follow this with a secondary line:

Your 7-day free trial has begun. Take a moment to explore what’s in your products.

Ensure:
	•	The welcome line is visually emphasized (slightly larger font than body text but smaller than the main title).
	•	The secondary line is lighter and supportive.

⸻

2. Improve Visual Hierarchy

Adjust layout so that content flows clearly in this order:
	1.	Icon / Brand marker
	2.	Title: “Welcome to Auraly+”
	3.	New welcome copy (primary emotional message)
	4.	Secondary trial message
	5.	Feature list card (Auraly+ benefits)
	6.	Pricing / reassurance text
	7.	Primary CTA
	8.	“No credit card required” reassurance

Ensure spacing visually separates these sections.

⸻

3. Enhance Feature List Presentation

Convert the “Auraly+ offers” section into a visually distinct card:
	•	Slightly tinted background (subtle green/neutral)
	•	Soft rounded corners
	•	Light shadow or border

Inside the card:
	•	Replace plain checkmarks with meaningful icons:
	•	📷 Unlimited scans
	•	⚙️ Personalized ingredient sensitivity settings
	•	🕓 Full scan history and insights

Add a subtle label above the card:

✨ Included with Auraly+

⸻

4. Refine Pricing Copy Formatting

Keep existing pricing text, but reformat it for readability:

Text:

After your trial, the choice is yours:
Upgrade to Auraly+ for $4.99 per month, or continue with Auraly Basic and enjoy 5 free scans each month.
No surprises, no hidden charges.

Formatting:
	•	Break into multiple lines
	•	Italicize or soften the “No surprises…” line
	•	Reduce visual weight slightly compared to the feature card

⸻

5. Update Primary CTA

Change the main button text to:

Start My Trial →

Button should:
	•	Be visually prominent
	•	Use brand accent color
	•	Have subtle hover / tap feedback

⸻

6. Emphasize “No Credit Card” Reassurance

Move “No credit card required” directly beneath the CTA.
	•	Smaller font
	•	Muted color
	•	Optional lock/shield icon:
🔒 No credit card required

⸻

7. Add Subtle Engagement Nudges

Optionally:
	•	Add a small badge near the feature card: “Recommended for new users”
	•	Add a gentle fade/slide-in animation on modal load (very subtle)

⸻

8. Constraints
	•	Do not add aggressive sales language.
	•	Keep tone calm, respectful, and transparent.
	•	Preserve existing functionality — only update layout, copy, and visual hierarchy.
	•	Do not introduce additional steps or friction.

⸻

Success Criteria:
	•	Users immediately understand they are receiving a free premium experience.
	•	Value of Auraly+ is visually clear within 3 seconds.
	•	Pricing feels transparent and non-threatening.
	•	CTA feels safe and inviting to click.
