Revely Design System v1

A field guide, not a dashboard.

Revely has exactly one moment that matters: the second between pointing the camera and getting an answer. This system spends everything on that second — warm paper, ink type, and colour reserved so strictly for meaning that a verdict lands like a stamp.

Semantic ink — the other 10%

These three inks are the only saturated colour in the product. They may appear on a verdict card, an ingredient dot, a history tile edge, and nowhere else. Buttons are ink. Links are ink. Tab bars are ink. If a colour appears outside a verdict, it is a bug.

Screenshot rule
The card must be legible cropped to its top 40% — the region a person's thumb leaves visible when they film a scan. That means verdict word, reason sentence, profile chip and mark all live above the fold, and the card carries its own paper background rather than inheriting the camera behind it.

Export rules
01Always light theme, always the paper ground. It has to survive being posted next to white UI.
02Feed crop leads with word + reason + bilingual block. Story crop leads with the seal, because it's read at arm's length.
03Reason copy switches to first person on export — "my three allergens", not "your". The poster is speaking, not the app.
04Never include the product photo. It drags in packaging trademarks and it's the label reading, not the pack, that's impressive.
05Profile chip is never stripped, but shows allergen names only — no profile name unless the user opts in.

05

Motion & haptics

The reveal is the product's signature. Total budget from shutter to legible verdict: under 900ms of motion, however long the network takes underneath.

{{ m.t }}
{{ m.name }}
{{ m.desc }}
{{ m.spec }}
Haptic vocabulary
Safe
One soft tap. impactLight as the card seats.
Caution
Tap, 90ms gap, lighter tap. warning notification.
Avoid
Sharp double. impactHeavy ×2, 60ms apart, fires before the card is readable.
Unsure
Nothing. Silence is the honest signal.
Rules that stop it feeling cheap
01The scan line sweeps once. If analysis is still running, the frame stays frozen with a mono status line — no looping animation.
02The card never fades in. It rises, with a shadow, over a frozen frame — that spatial move is what makes it feel like an object.
03The verdict word is already there when the card arrives. Never animate the word itself — no counting up, no typing.
04Only the seal animates after arrival: a 450ms scale-and-settle at −4°, delayed 200ms. One flourish, not five.
05Honour prefers-reduced-motion: card appears seated, seal static, haptics unchanged.

Delete on sight
Brand.primary #208AEF — there is no brand blue any more. Buttons are ink.
borderLeftWidth: 4 on every card — replaced by the 6px dot.
RiskLabels 'Low risk / Use caution / High risk' — becomes Safe / Caution / Avoid, and the sentence carries the specifics.
Gradient CTAs, Space Grotesk, Inter — from the old wellness guidelines.
The leaf mark, and every "Auraly" string — package name, deep-link scheme, share-sheet title, permission dialogs. A half-renamed app leaks the old name where you can't see it.
Build order for Claude Code
01Swap tokens in constants/design.ts, load the three fonts.

03Rebuild analysis-view.tsx as the verdict card in the order in §03.
